If you have already setup wifi on your Rubik Pi, you just need your Rubik Pi to be powered on and connected to wifi

To determine whether you are succesfully connected to wifi, follow the steps at this link:
https://www.thundercomm.com/rubik-pi-3/en/docs/rubik-pi-3-user-manual/1.0.0-u/set-up-your-device/

Once connected to wifi, follow these steps to setup SSH to VSCode:
1.	Click on >< in the bottom left of VSCode
2.	Click connect to host
3.	If you need to add a new host, the format is:
    a.	Ssh <username>@<ip address>
    b.	Ssh ubuntu@192.168.86.26
4.	You’ll be prompted to enter your password in the top search bar
