1.	Plug in raspberry pi camera
2.	Plug in micro usb to debug port but not into pc
3.	Plug in usb c power cord
4.	Solid green power light should come on
5.	Dark green debug light should flash once
    a.	If not, hold power button for a second or two
6.	Plug usb side of micro usb cord into pc
7.	Open PuTTY
8.	Serial
9.	Correct COM port
    a.	Check in device manager
10.	Baud rate to 115200
    a.	Set matching baud rate in device manager
